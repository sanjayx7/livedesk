Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What is SalesRoute.ai?
A: SalesRoute.ai is an AI-powered Sales Force Automation and Distribution Management System created by Technaureus Info Solutions. It helps businesses manage field sales, inventory, distributors, and business analytics.

---

Q: Which businesses can use SalesRoute.ai?
A: Any business involved in product sales, field sales, distribution, wholesale, retail, or inventory management can use SalesRoute.ai.

---

Q: Does SalesRoute.ai work offline?
A: Yes. The SalesRoute.ai mobile application works offline. Field representatives can take orders, record visits, and collect payments without internet. Data automatically syncs when internet connectivity is restored.

---

Q: Is there a mobile application?
A: Yes. SalesRoute.ai provides mobile applications for field sales representatives and managers on Android and iOS devices.

---

Q: Can managers track sales representatives in real time?
A: Yes. Managers can track live location, visit history, attendance, and activity reports of sales representatives.

---

Q: Can SalesRoute.ai manage inventory?
A: Yes. It includes multi-warehouse stock management, batch tracking, stock transfers, and real-time inventory reports.

---

Q: Does it support distributors?
A: Yes. SalesRoute.ai supports distributor management, retailer management, and dealer management.

---

Q: Does it provide reports and analytics?
A: Yes. It provides sales reports, inventory reports, collection reports, attendance logs, and AI business analytics.
